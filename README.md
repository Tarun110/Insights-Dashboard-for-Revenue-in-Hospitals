# 🏨 Revenue Insights in Hospitality Domain

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze hotel revenue performance, occupancy trends, and booking behavior across multiple properties. The solution empowers hospitality stakeholders with data-driven insights to optimize pricing strategies, improve occupancy rates, and maximize overall revenue.

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of hospitality KPIs, enabling hotel management teams to monitor operational performance, compare property-level metrics, and identify revenue growth opportunities.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

* **Power BI** – Interactive dashboards and data visualization
* **DAX** – Advanced KPI calculations and time intelligence measures
* **Power Query** – Data transformation and preparation
* **Excel / CSV** – Data storage and staging
* **Data Modeling** – Relationship management and analytical reporting

## 📊 Key Business Metrics

* Revenue
* RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)
* ADR (Average Daily Rate)
* DSRN (Daily Sellable Room Nights)
* Occupancy %
* Realisation %
* Cancellation Rate
* Customer Ratings

## ✨ Dashboard Features

### 📈 Revenue & Performance Overview

* Monitor overall revenue performance across properties
* Track RevPAR, ADR, Occupancy %, DSRN, and Realisation %
* Compare weekday and weekend performance trends

### 🏙️ Dynamic Filtering & Segmentation

* Analyze hotel performance by city and room category
* Enable flexible drill-down analysis for stakeholders

### 📅 Time-Series Analysis

* Visualize weekly performance trends across multiple KPIs
* Identify seasonality patterns and booking fluctuations

### 🏨 Property-Level Performance Comparison

* Compare operational and financial metrics across hotel properties
* Evaluate ADR, occupancy, cancellation rates, and guest ratings

### 🌐 Booking Platform Analytics

* Assess revenue contribution from direct, OTA, and offline channels
* Compare platform-wise ADR and realization percentages

### 🍰 Revenue Distribution Analysis

* Understand revenue contribution by hotel category
* Visualize business versus luxury segment performance

## 🎯 Business Impact

This solution helps hospitality businesses:

* Optimize room pricing strategies
* Improve occupancy and revenue performance
* Identify high-performing properties and booking channels
* Reduce revenue leakage through cancellation analysis
* Support strategic decision-making with real-time insights
* Enhance profitability through data-driven operations
